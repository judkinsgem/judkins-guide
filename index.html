Judkins Park

Your Home Base

Judkins Park is a beloved Central District green space with sweeping views of Lake Washington, the Cascades, and Mount Rainier on clear days. It's a genuine neighborhood hub — locals bring dogs, kids, and lawn chairs. The park sits at 2150 S Norman St and is open daily from 6am–10pm.

⚽ Sports fields 🎾 Tennis courts 🏀 Basketball 🛝 Playground 🐕 Dog-friendly 🏔 Mountain views
Central Park Trail
Trail

A peaceful loop trail winding through the surrounding neighborhood. Great for a morning walk with views of the community and green hillsides. Easy, mellow terrain.

📍 2150 S Norman St 🕕 6am – 10pm
Leschi–Lake Dell Natural Area
Nature

A hidden woodland preserve just east of Judkins with forested trails and a beautiful viewpoint overlooking Lake Washington and Bellevue. Feels miles away from the city.

📍 3525 E Terrace St 🕕 6am – 10pm

Places to Eat

Wood Shop BBQ
BBQ · $$
★★★★★

The best BBQ in Seattle — no contest. Texas-style brisket, ribs, and pulled pork done with genuine skill. The chili brisket mac & cheese is a must. Often draws lines, worth every minute.

📍 2513 S Jackson St 🕙 11am – 8pm daily 📞 (206) 485-7381
Reckless Noodle House
Vietnamese · $$
★★★★★

A neighborhood gem for creative noodle dishes and killer cocktails. The Ma La Braised Beef Cheek Noodle and Five Spice Duck Confit are outstanding. Great date spot, buzzy atmosphere.

📍 2519 S Jackson St 🕓 4pm – 11pm (Fri–Sat till midnight) 📞 (206) 329-5499
Wally's NW Soul
Soul Food · $$
★★★★½

Vibrant and modern soul food spot with serious wings — crispy, juicy, with loads of flavor options. The mac & cheese is homemade and creamy. Say hi to Wally if he's around.

📍 2218 S Jackson St 🕛 Noon – 8pm (Sun till 6pm, Mon closed) 📞 (206) 693-3335
Simply Soulful
Soul Food · $$
★★★★

Black-owned neighborhood classic on S Jackson. Known for their "soul rolls," collard greens, catfish, and banana pudding. Counter service, casual, authentic. Breakfast and lunch on weekends.

📍 2321 S Jackson St 🕙 10am – 8pm (Fri–Sun from 9am, Mon closed) 📞 (206) 402-6618
Ba Bar Capitol Hill
Vietnamese · $$
★★★★½

Lively Vietnamese restaurant and cocktail bar just north on 12th Ave. Pho, bun cha, imperial rolls, and a full bar — open until midnight every night. Great after-work or late-night option.

📍 550 12th Ave 🕙 11am – midnight daily 📞 (206) 328-2030
Moonlight Cafe
Vietnamese · $
★★★★

A neighborhood Vietnamese staple on S Jackson with an impressively extensive menu — including a full separate vegan menu with plant-based versions of traditional dishes. Authentic egg rolls, pho, and a pancake worth every bite. Great value.

📍 1919 S Jackson St 🕙 10am – 8:50pm daily 📞 (206) 485-7145
Wonder Ethiopian Restaurant
Ethiopian · $$
★★★★

Incredible Ethiopian food with 100% teff injera, a huge veggie combo platter, and meat samplers big enough for the whole table. Also a sports bar — live jazz on Friday nights makes it one of the best evenings on the corridor.

📍 1800 S Jackson St 🕙 11am – 11pm (Fri–Sun till 1am, Tue closed) 📞 (206) 538-0044
Jackson Street Pizza Lounge
Pizza · $$
★★★★½

A charming neighborhood pizza lounge at the east end of S Jackson. Creative pies like the mushroom vegetarian with inventive cocktails to match. Warm staff, cute decor, and a genuinely fun local atmosphere. Evenings only.

📍 2901 S Jackson St 🕓 4pm – 10pm (Fri–Sat till 11pm) 📞 (206) 485-7118

Coffee & Pastries

Temple Pastries
Bakery · Café
★★★★½

Right on S Jackson — beautiful, inventive pastries that people drive across the city for. The miso caramel cookie, gruyère & shallot croissant, and kouign-amann are legendary. Bright, plant-filled space, lines move fast.

📍 2524 S Jackson St #104 🕖 7am – 3pm daily
Broadcast Coffee
Coffee
★★★★½

Shares the same building as Temple Pastries (they're sister spots). Solid espresso drinks with indoor and outdoor seating — a good spot to settle in for a work session or a meeting.

📍 2524 S Jackson St #104 🕖 7am – 3pm daily
The Usual
Café · Ukrainian
★★★★½

A charming, beautifully designed café on E Yesler with a unique Eastern European twist — syrnyky (Ukrainian cheese pancakes), pelmeni, RAF coffee, and borscht. One-of-a-kind in Seattle.

📍 1414 E Yesler Way 🕗 8am – 4pm (Sat–Sun till 6pm)
Franz Bakery Outlet
Bakery Outlet · $
★★★★½

A beloved Seattle institution right in the neighborhood. Deeply discounted bread, bagels, donuts, sourdough, and pastries direct from the Franz bakery. Spend over $10 and get a free loaf. A must for stocking up — prices are a fraction of grocery stores.

📍 2006 S Weller St 🕗 8am – 5pm Mon–Fri, 9am – 5pm Sat, closed Sun 📞 (206) 322-0931

Bars & Nightlife

Twilight Exit
Dive Bar · $
★★★★½

The quintessential Central District dive — dimly lit with lanterns, pinball machines in the back, and bartenders who pour fast and pour well. The food is surprisingly solid. Late nights, friendly crowd, great vibe.

📍 2514 E Cherry St 🕛 Noon – 1:30am daily 📞 (206) 385-2392
Neighbor Lady Public House
Pub · $$
★★★★½

A beloved neighborhood pub in Midtown Square with a lovely courtyard patio and excellent food. The chicken sandwich is exceptional. Don't miss Prime Rib Sundays — a 16oz cut for $25 with salad and potato.

📍 1133 24th Ave, Unit Q 🕓 4pm – midnight (Sat–Sun from 11am) 📞 (206) 420-4374
Red Lounge
Club · Caribbean
★★★★

Seattle's heart and soul for Afrobeats and Caribbean culture. Best oxtails in the city, jerk chicken, and beef patties. Fridays and Saturdays turn into a full-on dance night with a DJ. A truly unique spot.

📍 1212 E Jefferson St 🕐 1pm – 9pm (Fri–Sat till 2am, Mon closed) 📞 (206) 323-9112

Explore the Area

Washington Park Arboretum
Nature · Free
★★★★★

A stunning 230-acre botanical garden about a mile north. Forested trails wind past Japanese maples, towering redwoods, and lily-pad ponds. Gorgeous year-round; bloom season in spring is unforgettable.

📍 2300 Arboretum Dr E 🕗 8am – 8pm daily
S Jackson St Cultural Corridor
History · Walk

The stretch of S Jackson St between 20th and 26th Ave is one of Seattle's most historically significant Black cultural corridors. Stroll past murals, independent businesses, and history that shaped the city.

📍 S Jackson St, Central District 🚶 Free self-guided walk
Lake Washington Boulevard
Scenic · Bike/Walk

Head east about a mile and you hit the scenic Boulevard hugging Lake Washington — flat, beautiful, and perfect for biking or walking with mountain views across the water.

📍 Lake Washington Blvd S 🚲 Open daily, always free

Visitor Tips

Good to Know

  • Wood Shop BBQ and Temple Pastries both sell out — arrive before 1pm to be safe, especially on weekends.
  • The S Jackson St corridor is very walkable. Wood Shop BBQ, Reckless Noodle, and Temple Pastries are all within a single block of each other at 25th Ave.
  • Twilight Exit is cash-friendly and laid-back — no dress code, no attitude, just good drinks.
  • Neighbor Lady's courtyard patio catches afternoon sun and is one of the best outdoor drinking spots in the neighborhood.
  • For the Arboretum, park off Lake Washington Blvd E — the lot by the Japanese Garden fills up on weekends.
  • Rideshare (Lyft/Uber) is easy here. Street parking in the neighborhood is generally free and available outside of events at the park.
  • Seattle weather: always bring a light layer, even in summer. Rain can appear fast.
