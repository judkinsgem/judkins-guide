SEATTLE · CENTRAL DISTRICT Judkins Park Guest Guide Your neighborhood companion — food, drinks & things to do within a mile 🍖 Soul Food 🍜 Noodles & Cocktails ☕ Coffee & Pastries 🍺 Local Bars 🌿 Parks & Trails The Park Eats Coffee Bars Explore Tips 🌿 Judkins Park Your Home Base Judkins Park is a beloved Central District green space with sweeping views of Lake Washington, the Cascades, and Mount Rainier on clear days. It's a genuine neighborhood hub — locals bring dogs, kids, and lawn chairs. The park sits at 2150 S Norman St and is open daily from 6am–10pm. ⚽ Sports fields 🎾 Tennis courts 🏀 Basketball 🛝 Playground 🐕 Dog-friendly 🏔 Mountain views Central Park Trail Trail A peaceful loop trail winding through the surrounding neighborhood. Great for a morning walk with views of the community and green hillsides. Easy, mellow terrain. 📍 2150 S Norman St 🕕 6am – 10pm Leschi–Lake Dell Natural Area Nature A hidden woodland preserve just east of Judkins with forested trails and a beautiful viewpoint overlooking Lake Washington and Bellevue. Feels miles away from the city. 📍 3525 E Terrace St 🕕 6am – 10pm 🍽 Places to Eat Wood Shop BBQ BBQ · $$ ★★★★★ The best BBQ in Seattle — no contest. Texas-style brisket, ribs, and pulled pork done with genuine skill. The chili brisket mac & cheese is a must. Forested trails wind past Japanese maples, towering redwoods, and lily-pad ponds. Gorgeous year-round; bloom season in spring is unforgettable. 📍 2300 Arboretum Dr E 🕗 8am – 8pm daily S Jackson St Cultural Corridor History · Walk The stretch of S Jackson St between 20th and 26th Ave is one of Seattle's most historically significant Black cultural corridors. Stroll past murals, independent businesses, and history that shaped the city. 📍 S Jackson St, Central District 🚶 Free self-guided walk Lake Washington Boulevard Scenic · Bike/Walk Head east about a mile and you hit the scenic Boulevard hugging Lake Washington — flat, beautiful, and perfect for biking or walking with mountain views across the water. 📍 Lake Washington Blvd S 🚲 Open daily, always free 💡 Visitor Tips Good to Know Wood Shop BBQ and Temple Pastries both sell out — arrive before 1pm to be safe, especially on weekends. The S Jackson St corridor is very walkable. Wood Shop BBQ, Reckless Noodle, and Temple Pastries are all within a single block of each other at 25th Ave. Twilight Exit is cash-friendly and laid-back — no dress code, no attitude, just good drinks. Neighbor Lady's courtyard patio catches afternoon sun and is one of the best outdoor drinking spots in the neighborhood. For the Arboretum, park off Lake Washington Blvd E — the lot by the Japanese Garden fills up on weekends. Rideshare (Lyft/Uber) is easy here. Street parking in the neighborhood is generally free and available outside of events at the park. Seattle weather: always bring a light layer, even in summer. Rain can appear fast.
