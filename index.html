Judkins Park

Your Backyard

Judkins Park is right on your doorstep -- a beloved Central District green space with sweeping views of Lake Washington, the Cascades, and Mount Rainier on clear days. Locals bring dogs, kids, and lawn chairs. Open daily 6am to 10pm.

⚽ Sports fields 🎳 Skatepark 🎾 Tennis courts 🏀 Basketball 🛷 Playground 🐶 Dog-friendly 🏔 Mountain views
Central Park Trail
Trail

A peaceful loop trail winding through the surrounding neighborhood. Great for a morning walk with views of the community and green hillsides. Easy, mellow terrain.

📍 2150 S Norman St6am to 10pm daily
Leschi and Lake Dell Natural Area
Nature

A hidden woodland preserve just east of Judkins with forested trails and a viewpoint overlooking Lake Washington and Bellevue. Feels miles away from the city.

📍 3525 E Terrace St6am to 10pm daily

Explore Nearby

Washington Park Arboretum
Nature · Free
★★★★★

A stunning 230-acre botanical garden about a mile north. Forested trails wind past Japanese maples, towering redwoods, and lily-pad ponds. Gorgeous year-round -- spring bloom season is unforgettable.

📍 2300 Arboretum Dr E8am to 8pm daily
S Jackson St Cultural Corridor
History · Walk

One of Seattle's most historically significant Black cultural corridors -- right outside your door. Murals, independent businesses, and history that shaped the city.

📍 S Jackson St, Central DistrictFree self-guided walk

Scenic · Bike/Walk

Head east about a mile and you hit the scenic Boulevard hugging Lake Washington -- flat, beautiful, and perfect for biking or walking with mountain views across the water.

📍 Lake Washington Blvd SOpen daily, always free

Day Trips by Light Rail

The light rail puts a lot of Seattle's best attractions within easy reach. All of these are accessible from Judkins Park Station with no car needed. View the full Sound Transit schedule ↗

Pike Place Market
Downtown · Rail
★★★★★

Seattle's iconic public market overlooking Elliott Bay. Fresh seafood, local produce, the original Starbucks, artisan vendors, and the famous fish toss. Get there early on weekends. Take the rail to Westlake Station.

📍 85 Pike StDaily from 9am🚇 Westlake Station
Pioneer Square and First Thursday Art Walk
History · Art · Rail
★★★★½

Seattle's oldest neighborhood, about 10 minutes by car or a short rail ride to International District Station. Cobblestone streets, Victorian architecture, galleries, and great bars. On the first Thursday of every month, galleries across the neighborhood open their doors for a free self-guided art walk from 6 to 9pm -- one of the best free nights out in the city.

📍 1st Ave S, SeattleArt Walk: 1st Thursday, 6 to 9pm🚇 International District StationArt Walk info ↗
Seattle Art Museum (SAM)
Museum · Rail
★★★★½

World-class art museum in the heart of downtown with an impressive permanent collection and rotating exhibitions. Free first Thursdays for Seattle residents. Take the rail to University Street Station.

📍 1300 1st AveWed to Sun, 10am to 5pm🚇 University Street Station
Capitol Hill
Neighborhood · Rail

Seattle's most vibrant neighborhood is about 10 minutes by car or a quick rail ride. Packed with restaurants, bars, live music venues, coffee shops, and independent stores. Great for a full evening out.

📍 Broadway Ave E, Seattle🚗 About 10 min drive🚇 Capitol Hill Station
Olympic Sculpture Park
Art · Outdoors · Free
★★★★½

A free outdoor sculpture park on the waterfront with sweeping views of Elliott Bay and the Olympic Mountains. Connects to the Seattle Art Museum and a great waterfront walking path. Take the rail to Westlake and walk down.

📍 2901 Western AveOpen daily, always free🚇 Westlake Station
Seattle Aquarium
Attraction · Rail
★★★★

A fun waterfront aquarium on Pier 59 with Pacific Northwest sea life, octopuses, sea otters, and a newer ocean pavilion. Great for families or a rainy day. Take the rail to Westlake Station.

📍 1483 Alaskan Way, Pier 59Daily 9:30am to 6pm🚇 Westlake Station
Beneath the Streets Underground Tour
History · Rail
★★★★½

A guided walking tour through Seattle's hidden underground -- the original street level buried after the Great Fire of 1889. Fascinating history, great guides, about an hour long. Take the rail to International District Station.

📍 102 Cherry StDaily from 10:30am🚇 International District Station

Good to Know

Visitor Tips

  • The light rail station is a 4-minute walk through the park -- the easiest way to reach Capitol Hill, downtown, Pioneer Square, or the airport without a car.
  • Wood Shop BBQ and Temple Pastries both sell out -- arrive before 1pm, especially on weekends.
  • The S Jackson St corridor is very walkable. Wood Shop BBQ, Reckless Noodle, and Temple Pastries are all within a block of each other around 25th Ave.
  • Wonder Ethiopian has live jazz on Friday nights -- a great reason to go that specific evening.
  • Pioneer Square First Thursday Art Walk is completely free and a fantastic night out -- galleries open 6 to 9pm on the first Thursday of every month.
  • Franz Bakery Outlet closes at 5pm and is closed Sundays. Spend over $10 and you get a free loaf.
  • Slightly Furry is a welcoming, low-key spot -- order a flight and let the staff guide you.
  • Big John's PFI has free parking underneath -- easy to miss if you do not know it is there.
  • Neighbor Lady's courtyard catches great afternoon sun -- one of the best spots for a drink outside.
  • Seattle weather: always bring a light layer, even in summer. Rain can appear fast.
